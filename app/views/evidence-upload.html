Provide supporting evidence

Uploading evidence helps the security team investigate the incident faster. If you do not have evidence right now, you can skip this step and continue.

You can upload:

  • screenshots of error messages, system logs, or misdirected emails
  • email exports (.msg or .eml files)
  • photos of physical security breaches (such as damaged hardware or unattended files)

File limits: PDF, PNG, JPG, TXT, CSV, or MSG up to 10MB. Do not upload password-protected or .zip files.
